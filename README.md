A make.fun entry for [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3). The challenge: take one small, ordinary moment and turn it strange in a single sentence of no more than 200 characters.

## The sentence

It is 2 a.m., the kitchen is dark, and then the fridge door opens: a small bulb flares like a sunrise made just for you, and for a heartbeat the leftovers waiting inside are convinced morning has come.

## How to run

`node main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly one sentence, no trailing newline, well inside the 200-character gate.
